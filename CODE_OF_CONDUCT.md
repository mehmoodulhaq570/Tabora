# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Tabora is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to create an environment in which everyone feels valued, respected, and able to contribute their best work.

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions: GitHub issues and discussions, pull requests, code reviews, commit messages, documentation, and any other communication channels used by the project.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

We encourage community members to demonstrate the following qualities:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members
- Help and support fellow contributors
- Assume good intent in all interactions
- Take responsibility for mistakes and apologize when needed

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or any other protected characteristic
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Abuse of power or position
- Threats of violence or violent language directed at another person
- Spam or unsolicited promotion
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They will:

- Monitor community spaces and communications
- Address concerns promptly and fairly
- Take appropriate action in response to violations
- Maintain confidentiality of reporters
- Explain decisions and actions taken

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (repository, issues, discussions, pull requests)
- Official community events and channels
- Public spaces where an individual is representing the project
- Any interactions related to the Tabora project

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Report to Maintainers**: Contact project maintainers directly through private channels
2. **Provide Details**: Include specific information about the incident
3. **Stay Safe**: Do not engage in prolonged disputes with violators
4. **Confidentiality**: Your report will be handled confidentially

When reporting, please include:

- Description of the behavior
- Who was involved (if safe to disclose)
- When and where the incident occurred
- Any relevant context or links
- Your contact information

## Handling Violations

Project maintainers will investigate reports in a timely manner. Actions may include:

1. **Private Discussion**: Reminding community members of expectations
2. **Public Warning**: Formal notice that behavior is unacceptable
3. **Temporary Suspension**: Removal from community spaces for a set period
4. **Permanent Ban**: Removal from the community

Decisions will be made based on:

- Severity and context of the behavior
- Previous violations by the individual
- Intent and willingness to change
- Impact on the community

## Appeal Process

Individuals who have faced disciplinary action may appeal by:

1. Submitting a written appeal to project maintainers
2. Explaining why they believe the decision should be reconsidered
3. Demonstrating understanding of how their behavior violated the Code
4. Proposing how they will engage differently going forward

Appeals will be reviewed by project leadership and a decision will be communicated within 30 days.

## Guidelines for Constructive Engagement

### When Disagreeing

- Focus on ideas, not individuals
- Assume good intent
- Ask clarifying questions
- Provide evidence for your viewpoint
- Be open to changing your mind
- Use "I" statements ("I think..." rather than "You always...")

### Providing Feedback

- Be specific and actionable
- Frame feedback constructively
- Offer solutions, not just criticism
- Acknowledge effort and positive aspects
- Choose appropriate channels and timing
- Ask permission before critiquing publicly

### Responding to Criticism

- Listen without becoming defensive
- Thank people for feedback
- Ask clarifying questions
- Respond thoughtfully, not reactively
- Take time to process if needed
- Follow up if changes are made

## Community Values

Our community is built on:

- **Respect**: Value each person and their contributions
- **Inclusion**: Welcome people of all backgrounds
- **Collaboration**: Work together toward common goals
- **Transparency**: Be honest and open in communication
- **Growth**: Support learning and improvement
- **Quality**: Maintain high standards for the project
- **Safety**: Ensure everyone feels secure and respected

## Zero Tolerance

Zero tolerance applies to:

- Violence or threats of violence
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Harassment based on protected characteristics
- Doxxing or publishing private information
- Hate speech

Violations will result in immediate removal and potential legal action.

## Recognition

We appreciate and acknowledge:

- Community members who help enforce this Code of Conduct
- Those who report violations in good faith
- Contributors who model positive behavior
- Everyone who helps create a welcoming community
